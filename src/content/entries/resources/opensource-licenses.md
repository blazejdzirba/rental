“Free” never means “do whatever”. Every tool has a license. Short guide.

## Three colours I use

- **Green (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD)** — use commercially, modify, even sell. Keep attribution.
- **Yellow (GPL, AGPL, LGPL, MPL)** — you may **run** it. If you distribute modified code, share your changes under the same terms. AGPL also “sees” through the network. Practical rule: use as a service for yourself; don’t embed in a product you sell without advice.
- **Red (no license)** — all rights reserved. You can read and learn. Don’t copy.

## For a small business

1. **MIT PMS** — run it for your units, change the logo, even operate it for other hosts.
2. **AGPL tool (e.g. FreeScout)** — self-host and use. Don’t ship its code inside a paid product.
3. **Scrapers** — license may be MIT; the **site ToS** is often the real risk.

## Quick chooser

| You want to… | Look for |
|---|---|
| Run it inside your company | any green license |
| Embed in a product for customers | MIT / Apache |
| Modify and keep changes private (as SaaS) | careful with AGPL |
| Learn from the code only | anything, including red |

When unsure: read `LICENSE`, then [choosealicense.com](https://choosealicense.com/).
