Today I closed a bookmarks folder named `try-later`. It had been growing since March.

The rule I’m trying: **if it’s worth keeping, it gets an entry on this site.** Title, why I care, how to run it, link out. Everything else can go.

Collecting feels productive. Publishing a short note is actually useful — to future-me, and maybe to someone else.
